# ADR-0015 — The project's journey on the site, in English and Portuguese

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: **accepted** by the maintainer on 2026-10-17. Estimated effort: S (pages written; upkeep S per batch).
Amends ADR-0010 (English everywhere) by one exception; ADR-0010 otherwise stands.

## Context

The maintainer asked for a lasting way to show the project's evolution "from the beginning, and
keep updating", to reach researchers, academics, engineers and community members, and stated on
2026-10-17 that outreach is done in English and in Portuguese. A study of how comparable projects
do it (kernel and systems projects, agent-memory projects; kept outside the repository, as
`AGENTS.md` asks for outreach material) found three things this repository can carry itself:

- a timeline of milestones linked to the journals (the journals are the project's strongest
  material, and nobody reads 118 of them to find the turns);
- a status page answering what the kernel asks of a new filesystem
  (`Documentation/filesystems/adding-new-filesystems.rst`): tests, maintainers, users, what is
  missing;
- a page of open research topics for universities, and one on how the project is made (one
  maintainer, AI coding assistants, the journal as the trail of human review).

ADR-0010 made the repository English-only, with `README.pt-BR.md` as the one courtesy mirror. A
Brazilian audience (universities, student kernel groups, the local community) is half of the
people the maintainer wants to reach.

## Decision

- Four pages join the site: `site/journey.html`, `site/status.html`, `site/research.html` and
  `site/how.html`, each with a Portuguese counterpart under `site/pt/`, linked both ways and
  marked with `hreflang`. The existing pages gain a "Journey" link.
- They are generated by `scripts/site-journey.py`, which holds the content of both languages side
  by side, so a milestone or a status answer is never updated in one language only. Links point
  to the journals and ADRs on GitHub; journal titles and contents stay in English.
- **Exception to ADR-0010:** these pages and `README.pt-BR.md` are the Portuguese text in the
  repository. Journals, ADRs, specifications, code and comments stay English-only; the
  Portuguese pages translate, they never say something the English pages do not.
- These pages are project documentation, not outreach: drafts of posts, messages, calls for
  talks and research about people stay outside the repository.
- Upkeep: a new milestone, status answer or research topic is added in the same batch that
  makes it true, in both languages.

## Alternatives considered

- **A separate site outside the repository.** Keeps ADR-0010 untouched, but splits the project's
  public face in two and puts the timeline out of reach of the review that keeps the journals
  honest. Rejected for the timeline and status; it stays the right place for posts.
- **Translate the journals.** Rejected for the reason ADR-0010 gives: two copies drift.
- **English only.** Rejected by the maintainer's statement of 2026-10-17.

## Consequences

- `scripts/check-site.py` checks the new pages' links like the others (12 pages).
- Every batch that changes the status (tests, maintainers, users) updates `site-journey.py`.
- If a third language is ever asked for, the generator takes it; the exception would then need a
  revision of this ADR.
